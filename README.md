front page — COP31 collapses with no deal, strikes hit Bahrain, flats are
hit in the Kyiv region, Democrats take the US House, Gaza is 99% toxic,
plus the Nvidia scandal, a Delta outage and "El Chapo Isidro" captured.

photos, no copied artwork). `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
